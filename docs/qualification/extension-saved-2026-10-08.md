# Saved extension candidates and explicit acceptance

Qualified Extension candidates now survive publication, Ready, explicit
acceptance, undo/redo and cold picture admission. Completion still leaves the
deterministic fallback unchanged. Normal app and headless generation still
allocate Bridge jobs; this record does not qualify an approved Extension pack
or its unfinished generation controls.

## Retained operation and dependencies

Document schema 47 retains `GeneratedSamplingMap::Bridge` or `Extension`.
Extension keeps direction, rational rates, context count K, generated count E
and original output count N. Accepted playback reads the materialized sampled
master. Copying and shortening preserve that exact map; shortening exposes its
prefix in both directions. For FromRight this removes the tail nearest the
conditioning side, without retiming or silently selecting a different suffix.

Store schema 74 refuses older unused development packages without modification.
Native receipts retain their exact operation plan and input shape. Extension
requires admission evidence, chronological context objects, explicit opposite
presence or absence, and continuity signatures. Protocol, completion,
controls and captured inputs must agree on operation and direction.

The store verifies every published dependency before Ready or acceptance.
One cooperative 60-second deadline governs a complete verification pass;
identical objects are copied and hashed once, and each private snapshot is
released before the next. Shared PNGs do not make another variant's output
accepted or immune to expiry. Portable copies and history retain all required
inputs, including the signatures.

`StoredGeneratedProvenance` dispatches from the authored operation without
trying another parser on failure. Extension admission binds the original
project, complete sampling map and native/sample/provenance identities, then
recomputes retained context and rejection policy. A copied Hold or later
revision need not match the old worker target. The common cold picture reader
verifies all conditioning identities and SHA-256 declarations, native bytes,
asset records and the decoded sampled contract. It needs no model or original
worker directory.

## Limits and remaining work

No new model inference, packaged app run, native window inspection or encoded
export is claimed by this increment. The real-media test proves saved admission
and exact picture preparation; worker loading claims remain synthetic. UI
coverage here is deterministic, with no claim of physical keyboard, VoiceOver
speech or visual quality qualification.

The independently approved provider capability, a longer measured generation
envelope, normal worker allocation and native Extension generation controls
remain required. Rejection-threshold calibration and real-person seam quality
remain on the owner's verification list under spec §29.1. DP-12 remains Partial.
