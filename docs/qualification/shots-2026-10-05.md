## Ground truth

Marked by hand. Contact sheets of every eighth picture located candidate
areas; every candidate (Homebrew FFmpeg 8 scene score above 0.04 and four
times its local median, luma ramps, anything visible on the sheets) was then
inspected picture by picture at 192 × 108 and, where unclear, at 480 × 270.
FFmpeg was used for inspection only.

Hard cuts (24): 347, 441, 671, 809, 881, 951, 1020, 1136, 1287, 1406, 1798 (to
black), 1845 (from black), 2063 (whip pan into a close-up; 2063 itself is a
one-picture blend), 2097, 2213, 2384, 2752, 2850 (to black), 2862 (from black,
credits), 2982 (credit page over the same background), 3102 (to black), 3126
(from black), 3388 (to black), 3427 (license text appears on black).

Gradual transitions (3):

| Pictures (half-open) | Kind | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 46..63 | Fade in | From 46 black pictures; luma ramps linearly. |
| 2117..2119 | Dissolve (2 pictures) | Through a white flash, llama to the Earth; shorter than the 3-picture minimum. |
| 2450..2464 | Dissolve | Night to day with unchanged framing. |

Not transitions, checked: 488–504 (a fence post sweeps past the lens), 976–985
(camera pull-back), 1440 (the llama leaves a static frame), 1200, 2233–2300 and
2653 (lightning, sparks, explosion), 2183–2193 (sun dims), 2296–2306 (fast tilt
up into a darkening sky with a continuous smoke trail).

## Determinism and resume

- Stored measures are byte-identical with 1, 4 and 8 decode threads (SHA-1 of
  the BLOB `3ace25de4a53cbb2…` each time). With frame threads FFmpeg logs
  "Late SEI is not implemented" 28 times per scan; one thread logs none.
- A one-thread scan was killed with `SIGKILL` after 12.5 s; its checkpoint
  held 2,399 of 3,504 pictures. The next `detect-shots` reported
  `resumed_from: 2399`, decoded 1,155 pictures (from 2,349), took 2.6 s,
  produced the same BLOB hash and deleted the checkpoint.
