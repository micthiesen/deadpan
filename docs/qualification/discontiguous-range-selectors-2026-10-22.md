# Discontiguous interval selectors

Status: implemented and locally verified within the scope below. This record
distinguishes each retained candidate; no requirement or gate is newly complete.

## Relative intent and finished geometry

`:select ranges=[{"start":2,"end":6},{"start":10,"end":14}]` selects
positive half-open intervals relative to one captured Edit cursor. The strict
wire admits 1 through 64 signed integer pairs in ordinary Your edit with Linked
role. Checked resolution validates every input against the captured ordinary
Sequence scope, sorts intervals and merges overlaps or touching endpoints.
There is no clamping or enclosing-range fallback. Finished selection retains
absolute, separated geometry while the cursor moves, including a one-interval
set's distinct authority. The read-only inspector lists every interval and the
sum of their durations; beat cards highlight the separate windows.

Recorded SelectRanges keeps the original relative pairs. Named calls resolve
them from their current entry cursor. A Visual Cut consumes the finished set;
dot requires a newly selected set instead of replaying old endpoints. Scalar
operators, unsupported temporal interiors, selection loops, AI generation,
saved framing and replacement refuse set authority. Place slice also refuses
when its copied source is an ordinary slice, so the range selection cannot be
silently ignored in favor of the cursor.

Partial endpoints use Source, Hold or supported fragments. Enter an ordinary
Group before slicing its interior; whole composite children remain eligible at
their exact seams. Existing single-range endpoint limits are not broadened.

## One capture, transaction and durable register

All windows are captured from one immutable entry document before reverse-order
DeleteRange leaves execute in one Compound. Capture admission permits uniformly
whole-child or uniformly range components, never a mixed wire. Range components
retain the exact common scope, parent, timing and capture revision. Repeated
authored context is legitimate when several windows share one Source, but every
shared record must agree and historical admission reconstructs every capsule.

Seam paste uses component-qualified identities and one shared logical mark pool.
Every distinct destination binding survives when disjoint windows share an
original owner. Independent ordinary pastes remain a different operation with
separate logical marks. Whole-node duplicate and ancestor rules are unchanged.
The existing EditedSet format needs no new document or database schema.
