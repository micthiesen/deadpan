# Ordered Original-owner EQ, 2026-10-11

This increment connects the finite EQ processor to a checked Original owner's
actual decoded history and its serialized memoryless treatment order. It does
not add an authored EQ field, command, native control or complete group mix.
DP-09 and DP-16 remain partial under the unchanged full specification.

## Implemented boundary

`AudioTreatmentBoundary` belongs to an immutable plan and owner occurrence.
It retains definition, gap and retained provenance. A whole Repeat can cross
plays; a child occurrence cannot impersonate another play. Boundary zero is
after escalation and before the first treatment; the final boundary is after
the last treatment. Invalid indices and foreign plans reject.

`PreparedOwnerEqualizer` uses the input's observed sample to select retained
history only when that history contains the exact requested owner. An ancestor
of a bound child continues to use its complete current context. Source media
is opened by the controlled reader, never the constructor.

The reader processes 1,024-sample tiles with 2,048 samples on each side, retaining
signed consumer labels. Raw time/pitch and creative edges feed descendant
treatments and the owner's prefix. EQ then feeds the suffix. Exterior owners
and independent sounds are excluded. Silence/exhaustion masks apply to output;
gain mute is evaluated only in its chosen prefix or suffix. Output remains f64
before mixing and mastering, without clipping or normalization.

## Remaining work and limits

The one-owner preparation API does not compose multiple EQ owners recursively,
mix independent sounds, solve independently timed group automation, or implement
per-voice send/tail permissions. The existing ordinary authored bus remains
unchanged. Filtered tiles are recomputed, so this is not an interactive latency
qualification. Fixed native FFT state remains outside PCM residency accounting,
as documented by `StageLimits`; tile and raw PCM share its bounded allowance.
