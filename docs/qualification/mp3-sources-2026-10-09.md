DP-02 / DP-16, specification §16.3. Raw mono/stereo MPEG-1/2/2.5 Layer III
sounds use the normal catalog, placement, history, audition and Render paths.
The native Add sound picker includes `.mp3`; qualification checks bytes rather
than the extension. This milestone does not complete the source-format and

## Admission and decoded time

A bounded sparse frame walk runs before FFmpeg allocation. It checks all nine
sample rates (8,000 through 48,000 Hz), frame lengths, packet/sample budgets,
stable rate and mono/stereo interpretation, and exact EOF. Bitrate and padding
may vary. There is no skipped junk or resynchronization. Optional Xing/Info
counts must match all physical audio frames and bytes, excluding the metadata
frame and ID3 tags. Untagged concatenations with identical framing cannot be
distinguished from one continuous stream; tagged count mismatches fail.

ID3v2.3/2.4 has zero header/frame transformation flags, at most 1 MiB, 1,024
frames, 64 KiB text/comment fields, and one inline bounded PNG/JPEG picture.
Audio ignores that picture without decoding it. ID3v1 is admitted at EOF.
Unknown/private/chapter payloads, unsynchronization, extensions, encryption,
compression, APE metadata, VBRI, free-format bitrate, emphasis, Layer I/II and
MP3 in other containers remain explicitly unqualified.

The pinned FFmpeg 8.0.3 `mp3float` decoder preserves float amplitude and actual
mono/stereo speaker identity. No new dependency or library is added. Raw MP3
headers do not provide FFmpeg's codec parameters until the first parsed packet;
opening retains that packet for normal decoding, including its skip side data.
It never calls `find_stream_info` or decodes the attached picture. Payload and
side data share the packet byte bound, and the first packet is counted once.
CRC-protected frames use `AV_EF_CRCCHECK | AV_EF_EXPLODE`.

The checked `mp3` receipt carries frame count, samples per frame, rate, channels
and exact declared trim. Physical samples are 1,152 per MPEG-1 frame and 576 per
MPEG-2/2.5 frame. Raw PTS and durations use FFmpeg's exact `1/14112000` time base.
Every decoded frame and raw skip record must match the admitted inventory.
Leading and trailing skips can span multiple frames and remain unavailable to
sample reads. Deserialization rechecks the complete contract.

For a recognized LAME/Lavf/Lavc encoder tag, the leading skip is the declared
encoder delay plus FFmpeg's 529 synthesis samples; terminal skip is the declared
padding minus those 529 samples. Insufficient padding, contradictory counts,
empty audible coverage or a checksum mismatch fails. Untagged files and unknown
encoder tags provide no qualified trim, so all physical samples remain audible.
No event alignment, guessed silence removal, tolerance increase or container
endpoint substitution runs.

The first mono-VBR probe failed the initial strict LAME checksum check. Inspection
of pinned `libavformat/mp3enc.c` showed that FFmpeg hashes a fixed 190-byte buffer
before writing the checksum, including zero buffer padding where necessary.
That differs from LAME's variable tag endpoint for mono/lower-rate files. The
guard accepts either exact checksum for Lavf/Lavc producers; other encoders do
not get that exception. The retained failure is `native-third.log`.

Primary implementation references are pinned FFmpeg 8.0.3's
`libavcodec/mpegaudiodecheader.c`, `libavcodec/mpegaudiodec_template.c`,
`libavformat/mp3dec.c`, `libavformat/mp3enc.c`, `libavformat/demux.c`, and
`libavformat/id3v2.c` in the qualified dependency's retained source directory.
They define the decoder's observed synthesis delay, manual skip behavior,
bitstream framing, metadata parser and checksum producer variant.

## Fixtures and focused checks

`native/deadpan-source/tests/generate_mp3_fixtures.py` creates 16 synthetic files
and independent development-FFmpeg `mp3float` PCM references. The manifest retains
producer versions, source WAV hashes, input/reference hashes, exact counts and
first/last frame observations. Signals are generated directly at each rate.
Fixtures include CBR, VBR, all nine sample rates, a 97-sample sound, untagged
physical audio, an unknown encoder tag, LAME CRC protection, ID3v2.3/2.4 cover art plus ID3v1, and declared
leading/trailing trim spanning several frames.

Native manual and ordinary decoding agree exactly. All 16 independent reference
comparisons pass fixed bounds of peak < 0.00001 and RMS < 0.000001. Ordinary tagged cases return exactly 8,197
samples per channel; the short case returns 97, untagged/unknown-encoder 10,368 and the explicit
multi-frame trim case 4,868. Native negative tests reject changing/truncated
frames, forged counts/checksums, invalid metadata, limits and corrupt audio CRC.
The media test reads the entire audible range, refuses both excluded boundaries,
round-trips receipts and rejects ten clock/trim receipt mutations per fixture.

The public CLI test registers and places six representative sounds without
changing picture time, prepares actual limited PCM, and exercises undo/redo and
complete project validation. Its initial harness failures used a path containing
`..`, an incorrect undo command and an obsolete expected revision after undo;
those were corrected in the harness without weakening product validation.
