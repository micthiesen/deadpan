# Jobs admission, queued generations and quitting

Source base `6dda3c47318e204093be08ef066fa6628591aea1`, Apple M5 Max,
macOS 26.5.2 (25F84), Rust 1.97.1, pinned FFmpeg 8.0.3.
This increment advances DP-18; it does not complete its remaining requirements.

## Behavior and review

Model admission observes battery, Low Power Mode, thermal pressure, memory
pressure and playback. Held rows explain their reason and offer Run anyway.
Up to four distinct pause generations can queue, with independent cancellation
and durable attempts. Progress-based remaining time is explicitly for the
current stage. Quitting with requested work offers waiting, cancellation or
returning to the editor; waiting retains a visible banner.

Independent review found a duplicate-target admission hole when the primary
generation slot emptied while a secondary slot remained live. Duplicate and
capacity validation now precede the empty-slot fast path. The service regression
test exercises that exact transition. Review also narrowed the subprocess audit:
fixed local power probes live in `system_conditions`, and the scripted worker
launch lives in a test/harness-only module. No production service file is broadly
exempted from the audit. Follow-up review found no further issues.

## Scope

Injected conditions and scripted generation exercise real admission, service
transactions, cancellation and durable attempt handling. They do not establish
real inference interference, physical power transitions, sleep behavior or
historical estimates. See [remaining Jobs work](../JOBS.md#remaining).
