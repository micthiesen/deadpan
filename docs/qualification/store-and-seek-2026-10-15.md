## Deferred keyframes

Writing a keyframe (serializing, storing and hashing the complete document)
moved out of the edit's transaction: a commit at depth 64 stays a patch and
leaves the keyframe due, and `ProjectStore::write_due_keyframe` converts the
head's row afterwards in its own transaction, recomputing the history
receipt's last link from a proved in-memory link. A commit still stores the
keyframe itself at depth 128 (or when its size bound exceeds the document
limit), and a writable open converts a due head. `perf edit` calls
`write_due_keyframe` after each edit's refresh, as an idle host would, and
reports it separately. The full design, invariants and crash-safety argument
are in [timing storage](../TIMING_STORAGE.md#deferred-keyframes).

### Tests

- `crates/deadpan-store/tests/revision_storage.rs`:
  - `keyframes_bound_every_chain_and_rebuild_every_revision` now writes each
    due keyframe after its commit and still requires exactly the previous
    placement (initial revision and every 64th), every bound, every
    revision rebuilt and receipt-proved, and rejection of a changed keyframe;
  - `due_keyframes_wait_for_the_host_within_the_hard_bound`: 200 revisions
    without writing any: depth `index % 128`, keyframes only at 0 and 128,
    every revision rebuilt, `validate_full`, receipt-proved read-only and
    writable opens, a read-only open writes nothing, a writable open
    converts the head (depth 71) and changes no other row, and the next
    commit has depth 1;
  - `a_deferred_keyframe_is_covered_by_the_receipt`: a converted keyframe
    is receipt-proved, and changing it fails both opens.
- `lib.rs` `a_due_keyframe_needs_a_proved_receipt_link`: a missing link, a
  wrong previous chain value and a wrong revision count each refuse the
  conversion without changing any row or the receipt; the proved link then
  converts it and a read-only reopen proves it and replays fully.
- `tests/failpoints.rs` `crashes_while_writing_a_due_keyframe_keep_a_valid_history`:
  a writer aborted at `keyframe-before-commit` and `keyframe-after-commit`
  (during a writable open's conversion) leaves the elided or converted head,
  both receipt-proved on read-only reopen (`replayed` 0); a writable reopen
  converts, `validate_full` passes before and after another commit.
- `tests/chaos_kills.rs`
  `random_process_kills_around_deferred_keyframes_never_corrupt_the_history`:
  8 seeded SIGKILLs of a writer committing pauses on a 240-Hold document
  and writing due keyframes after every third commit (so due heads also
  receive commits); every reported revision rebuilds, the head is the last
  reported or the cut-off commit, a writable reopen leaves nothing due, and
  the complete history replays. The regression run reported 4 deferred
  keyframes among 578 reports.
- The complete `cargo test -p deadpan-store --locked` passes (42 test
  binaries and doctest groups), and strict Clippy (`--all-targets -D warnings`) passes for
  `deadpan-store` and `xtask` and for the `deadpan-cli` `perf` example.

## Page-cache-cold seek

### Method

`cargo xtask perf --stages cold-seek` copies each media package once onto a
new APFS disk image, then for each of 10 samples attaches the image at a
fresh mount, runs `perf cold-seek`, and detaches it. `perf cold-seek` sizes
its Metal target from another copy of the package, then opens a progressive
preview session on the mounted copy (store, plan, verified private Original
snapshot, decoder) and measures to the first picture at a random frame,
decode plus Metal completion, as the `seek` stage's `cold.progressive` does.
It then repeats the same in the same process at another random frame, now
page-cache-warm.

Coldness checks (the `mincore` residency of each file, with a small
scratch tool; 16 KiB pages):

- after a fresh attach, before the sample: `project.sqlite` 0/69,
  `manifest.json` 0/1 and the Original 0/9,628 pages resident; the image's
  backing file 0/32,768 resident;
- after one cold sample: the database and the whole Original (9,628/9,628)
  resident, and the backing file still 0/32,768. The disk-image driver reads
  its backing file uncached, so nothing below the mount is cached either;
- reading a 2,000 MB file through a fresh mount took 0.435–0.460 s in three
  attach cycles (about 4.4 GB/s; 0.164–0.182 s, about 12 GB/s, when read
  again on the same mount). Repeated attaches did not get faster, so no
  cache survives a detach.

So each sample's first session really reads its database and Original from
the SSD, through the disk-image driver. A direct read from the internal
SSD's own volume could not be made cold without root and was not measured,
so neither its throughput nor its per-request latency is compared here.

### Results

Two complete runs of the after build (`cargo xtask perf --stages
edit,cold-seek` and `--stages cold-seek`), 10 samples per fixture each, ms.
The machine never reached the load gate (the suite waited 10 minutes and
flagged both stages), so every row is INFO, not a verdict.

| Fixture, run | Load per sample | Cold first picture p50 / max | Warm repeat, same process, p50 / max | Cold minus warm, paired median | Cold: session open / first decode / Metal, medians |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| gen-1080p60, run 1 | 5.0–8.3 | 204 / 381 | 234 / 514 | −22 | 34 / 158 / 17 |
| gen-1080p60, run 2 | 5.4–9.8 | 196 / 414 | 176 / 284 | +22 | 37 / 149 / 13 |
| gen-4k30, run 1 | 4.6–10.7 | 303 / 598 | 301 / 511 | +65 | 20 / 288 / 24 |
| gen-4k30, run 2 | 2.9–6.1 | 294 / 633 | 299 / 439 | +37 | 20 / 294 / 26 |

The background index measurement then verified the whole Original after
16–23 s (median per run), far longer than at load 3 because it is
single-threaded and competed for cores.

What this shows:

- A cold cache adds little to a first picture: the paired difference
  between each cold sample and the warm repeat in the same process (−22 to
  +65 ms median) is smaller than the spread between samples at these loads.
  Reading the database and the needed part of a 120–150 MB Original from
  storage at about 4.4 GB/s costs tens of milliseconds; decoding to the
  random frame dominates (149–294 ms median under this load).
- Under load 3–10 no fixture meets the §25 target at every sample: 1080p60's
  median is about 200 ms but its slowest samples are 381–414 ms, and the
  4K median is at the 300 ms limit with slowest samples of 598–633 ms. The
  2026-10-06 session-cold, page-cache-warm samples at load 2–3 were 125 /
  149 ms (1080p60) and 193 / 275 ms (4K) p50 / p95, and the warm repeats
  here are 1.4–1.6 times those, so this run's misses are mostly load. A
  gated run (every sample below load 3) is still needed for a PASS or FAIL.


## Not covered

- The native project service does not call `write_due_keyframe` yet
  (`crates/deadpan-app`, owned by other work); until it does, the app's
  keyframe stays due until the commit at depth 128 stores it inline or a
  writable open converts it. The proposed hook is the service loop's idle
  receive timeout.
- A request that arrives while a deferred keyframe is being written waits
  for it (about 47 ms on this fixture); nothing measures how often a user
  edit lands in that window.
- Physical power loss during the keyframe transaction (only process kills
  and aborts are tested).
- Cold seek from an Original stored directly on the internal SSD without the
  disk-image driver (that needs root to purge the cache), lower hardware
  tiers and network or external volumes.
- The remaining whole-document passes on a wide root (pause 65.6 / 75.5 ms
  here) and the first pause's binding of every Hold (160 ms).
