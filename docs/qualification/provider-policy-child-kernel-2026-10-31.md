## Construction and semantics

The child allocates span storage and copies newly owned asset identities, definitions,
instances, Repeat identities and headers only after their corresponding reservations.
Existing collection growth retains old and replacement buffers together. Values
precede their independent leases on disposal. Projected bills its retained Arc-reference
operation; the preexisting projection graph is outside this construction claim.

Empty Source/Silence current queries retain canonical0; Projected retains canonical1,
including empty queries. Empty Projected policy succeeds with sufficient admission
work; nonempty policy retains the later `current policy retained processing` error.
Invalid limits precede RequiresOwnedInput rejection. Queries use the supplied
PointCeil or RoundEven grid. Canonical fees remain literal; additional construction
work is separate and accepted units are forwarded once.

Source output completes current inventory, then completes its actual carrier query
and Leaf validation before emitted-Hold fees or ranges. Carrier Original content,
endpoints, bindings and issuer identities never enter the independent voice inventory.
The carrier borrows the actual signal without an allocating provider clone. Selecting
it before requesting child limits preserves Source input at its measured exact work
boundary. Empty Source output still admits the carrier's real headers.
