## Current delivery proof

The plan now compiles a separate weak proof for a complete, ordered, adjacent
forest of transparent Partition Retimes. Each full context must have explicit
matching copy provenance and a complete correspondence anchored to the event's
recorded historical owner path. Every piece must contain the corresponding
sound with the same source recipe and earliest clock contract. Current lineage
or equal shapes alone cannot establish continuity.

The token includes the first current Partition, concrete enclosing Repeat path,
current consuming context and proven historical event. Independent copied
forests and different plays cannot share it. It joins only already prepared
current routes; each keeps its support, source admission, gates and treatments.
It is never used to bind historical plans or expose hidden processing input.
Root processing contexts still fail historical remapping outside their journals.

Review found that failed structural attempts were not charged. Core now reports
consumed work on success and rejection, including late mismatches; compilation
charges it before another attempt. Both proof compilation phases share the
existing 100,000-unit allowance. Family comparisons charge their actual bounded
identity bytes instead of assuming every identifier occupies its maximum length.
No processing limit, PCM comparison or effect was weakened.

their work checks. Four focused tests cover exact cancellation around saturation,
mute, dynamic range boundaries, budgets and overflow. Review found no additional
issue. This optimization also does not resolve the full matrix's work failures;
the remaining actual preparation work needs another measured diagnosis.
