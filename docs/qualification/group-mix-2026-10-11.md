# Shared nonlinear group mixing, 2026-10-11

Saturation now processes the sum at its structural owner. Previously the
Original and each independent sound passed through the owner's saturation
separately, then their distorted outputs were added. Those operations differ.
The shared preview/export worker now builds bounded nested buses, sums each
owner's contributions, applies its serialized treatments, then feeds its parent.

Original sound and beat-owned sounds join their own structural paths.
Independent root sounds join only the root. Child balance precedes parent
saturation; gain can precede or follow saturation as authored. Repeat
occurrences remain distinct, with exact owner clocks and escalation. Linear
stretches keep the existing Q32 dB accumulation. No timing, layout, schema or
native command change is involved.

Routing, clock agreement and f64 PCM residency are admitted before media access.
All voices still undergo source admission, including muted and gated voices.
Overlapping contributions must agree on the exact affine clock of a shared
owner. Parents reduce after their children, with stable occurrence ordering for
sibling sums. Nonfinite input, sums and output fail explicitly; the final bus
converts to f32 before the existing common limiter. Raw edge-faded inspection
continues to exclude treatments.

## Actual export

catalog WAV and a quieter independent root copy of that WAV. The group has
12 dB saturation followed by -12 dB gain; the root has 6 dB saturation followed
by -6 dB gain. At an overlapping 256-sample window, the limited preview matches
an independently calculated two-channel waveform with maximum absolute error
**1.451007e-8**. The reference comes from the generated tone equation and the
