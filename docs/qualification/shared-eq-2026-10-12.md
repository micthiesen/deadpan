# Shared sound and group EQ, 2026-10-12

The authored bus now processes stored EQ with Original audio, root SoundEvents
and beat-owned attachments. Limited audition and Render use this path instead
of rejecting every EQ/independent-sound combination. Native EQ authoring remains
open; typed semantic and direct treatment commands already store the recipes.

## Shared stage order and policy

`shared_mix` routes each contribution through its ordered owner chain. Each
nonlinear or temporal owner receives one f64 sum. The EQ graph recursively reads
the complete prefix before its selected stage, filters it, then applies the
suffix. Saturation therefore processes the shared sum on its authored side of
EQ. Child output and parent intermediates retain f64 until final conversion.

Original and independent sounds retain their separate full input contexts.
Saved sound journals, current Hold gates, event edges/gain and checked stable
occurrences remain on the existing preparation path. EQ tiles use fixed signed
sample coordinates and include hidden crop history; current delivery remains a
separate gate. A root sound enters only the root, so child EQ cannot process it.

Every bus sample retains a bounded contribution permission set. An EQ tile
re-evaluates its complete shared prefix using the voices permitted at the
consuming sample. Its cache key includes that permission set. An allowed sound
under a silent Hold cannot release filtered history from a forbidden Original
or peer. This does not distribute nonlinear saturation across individual voices.
Authored gain/mute remains at its program position rather than becoming a
permission change.

An exactly neutral EQ remains serialized but executes as an identity on the
current input. A new regression exposed why this matters: expanding source
history for an identity processor changed the resampling at a saved NTSC crop
edge. The fixed path preserves those delivery samples bit for bit. Nonneutral
filtering separately retains complete saved input and its original copy anchor.

One request owns its nodes, filters, permission-keyed tiles and dependencies.
Voice count, graph depth, work, prepared frames and PCM/permission residency are
bounded. Sound preflight and mixer admission precede media reads; filter and
PCM reservations are restored on failure. Identity includes checked owner clock,
stage and parent context. Different audible automation on independently timed
shared inputs still returns a specific unsupported error.
