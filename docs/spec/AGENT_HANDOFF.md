### Decisions the specification leaves open

Confirm each with the owner or adopt the recommendation, then record it in
`DEADPAN_SPEC.md` and `docs/SPEC_PROVENANCE.md` before implementing.

1. **`audio-shift` (§6.5, "a separate explicit command is required to ripple
   audio independently").** Recommended: `:audio-shift +D|-D` over a Visual
   range inside one beat moves that beat's linked sound later or earlier by D
   relative to its picture and ripples only the independent root sound bus after
   the range by D; pictures and later picture time stay fixed; a shift that
   would leave the beat's measured audio support refuses; recorded as one
   semantic instruction. Implement on the existing `audio_offset` and root-route
   journal (`RootSoundOperation`), not a new clock.
2. **Video-only repeat policies (§6.5 "explicit fit/trim/hold policy").**
   `overflow=trim` exists. Recommended: `extend=hold` for video mirrors the
   audio form (`role_repeat.rs`): insert one silent pause after the beat for the
   overflow and continue the looping cutaway over it; `fit` is not added unless
   the owner wants loops time-compressed, which needs a cutaway rate the plan
   does not have.
3. **Original-pane operators (DP-10 "Original operators with word motions").**
   Today `y` in the Original pane copies the moment selection immediately.
   Recommended: in Original Normal mode `y` becomes an operator: `y` plus a
   word, sentence, pause or shot motion or object copies that Original span as
   a moment (same register path as `v`...`y`), and `yy` copies a finished
   moment selection. This changes a binding: run the keymap compatibility audit
   (`docs/KEYMAP_COMPATIBILITY.md`), regenerate the routing baseline and update
   Help and replays together.
4. **Ranged and one-play zoom in macros (Section 8 coverage).** The ranged
   recommendation was adopted in specification §7.4 on 2026-10-18: record
   `target=center` or retained-center intent, resolve each invocation range
   against the staged document, and refuse target-sampled recording. The pure
   builder now lives in core; a shared host resolver supplies plan-evaluated
   pose and qualified picture geometry. One-play authority is implemented for
   whole-owner Zoom/Call under explicit Play choices at every Repeat level,
   with staged target/presentation mapping and native scoped receipts.

## Non-negotiable correctness points

`3riw` means three total plays. Repeat gaps occur only between plays. A Hold inserts exactly N project frames and resumes untouched original speech. Do not accumulate fractional-rate duration rounding. Jobs cannot overwrite newer edits. Accepted generated media remains usable without the model. Export snapshots cannot mix revisions. Cache cleanup cannot remove referenced originals or accepted artifacts.

Undo cannot erase the original identity or cross its initialization baseline.
Deleting all current beats does not make another video eligible. Importing sound
does not imply placing it, lengthening the edit or replacing original speech;
sound-event overlay requires its actual authored and mixing path. Do not relabel
a generic blank-picture audio beat as a placed effect. Maintain core structural
capability and current recovery while enforcing V1 through the optional profile
and native workflow. The current development-format policy above supersedes
the older migration requirements for retired unused formats.
