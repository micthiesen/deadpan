Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: concept sheet for a native macOS app icon and identity
Primary request: Design three tightly related, premium icon concepts for Deadpan, a keyboard-first structural video editor for turning one original video into odd, funny edits. The name suggests an absolutely expressionless face. Make the humor understated and the design feel like a serious creative tool.
Composition: one horizontal sheet with three generously spaced square app icons, each shown large and again very small underneath. Label the three alternatives only A, B, C in a quiet sans-serif. Clean off-white presentation background. No wordmark yet.
Direction: A is an expressionless cinematic frame, a soft lavender horizontal screen shape with two square charcoal eyes and a straight yellow timeline mouth. B is a sculptural lavender film-frame outline containing a deadpan pause-face with two short vertical lavender eyes and a yellow flat mouth. C uses two offset lavender edit tiles that make a single deadpan face with square charcoal eyes and a flat yellow mouth. All three use a restrained dark charcoal rounded-square macOS enclosure, with subtle material depth and consistent straight-on perspective. Give each a memorable clear silhouette. The face is an abstract editorial symbol, not an illustrated mascot.
Color palette: charcoal #17191D / #202329, lavender #C4B5FD, pale golden yellow #F6D365, subtle near-white edge highlights.
Style: high-end macOS creative application identity, bold simple geometry, meticulous optical alignment, soft shallow layered material, crisp continuous curves, clean edges. Strong at 16 pixels. Less decoration is better. Balanced generous padding.
Constraints: no play triangles, clapperboards, film reels, letter D monograms, scissors, glossy rainbow gradients, sparkles, noisy textures, emoji faces, smiles, outlines around every component, perspective tilt, watermark, or mock device. Do not imitate any existing brand.
