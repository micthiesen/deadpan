# Ripple edge Trim: audio review

Reviewed `next-trim-design.md` against the current binding, composite insertion/deletion and PCM paths. Read-only review; no Cargo or native execution.

## Conclusion

No concrete phase or suffix-rebasing defect found in the proposed algorithm. One old-tree capture, separate target/suffix windows, then target-only physical rebasing matches the current evaluator. Keep the In-extension PCM gate: existing physical-prefix tests hide the added prefix behind a Partition, so they do not prove audible extension through an earlier resume.

Two implementation details should become explicit acceptance criteria:

1. **Partition edges do not introduce a new fade.** A default `split::partition` wrapper is transparent to sampling support and envelope boundaries. A crop that keeps W unchanged retains the physical Source's old filtering support and fade progress; it does not fade its newly exposed cut edge. A changed W can change the Source endpoint/filter envelope. Test both cases, including authored Source Hard policies and coincident ancestor edges. Do not copy Source policies onto the wrapper or interpret the wrapper's default Automatic fields as new audible boundaries.
2. **Append before rebasing, from one capture.** Do not call `prepare_suffix` twice or capture after installing the new crop. Extract its owner/window installation logic so target and suffix share one captured layout, one aggregate entry counter and disjoint owner sets. Install `phase_only_layout` before appending references; prune only after both sets and the target translation are installed. A fresh current-placement record must not replace the old lattice or earlier reanchors.

## Why the binding path fits

- `audio_binding_lifecycle::capture_for_composite_insertion` retains existing lattices/resumes and separately collects current root placements, including dormant Sources, compact Repeat defaults/gaps and opaque nonunity Preserve output. It captures all previously unbound physical recipes. It requires the sound-detached document.
- `insert_time::composite::shifted_owners` walks later siblings at every ordinary Sequence ancestor and stops below a nonunity Preserve owner. Trim must call it with the slot **after** the target, while a separate target-only set names the physical Source. `append_steps` supports distinct windows from the same capture and charges the term/entry limits.
- `AudioReanchorStep::allocation_entry` uses old structural allocation, not old audible support. Thus an audio-dormant target still has an entry. `AudioBindingState::resolve_owner` applies steps chronologically and accumulates the difference of independently rounded old sample boundaries. Hidden allocations with no entry preserve their previous map.
- `OwnedAudioBinding::rebase_local(p)` subtracts p from every placement's `reference_local_offset`, adds p to resume/phase endpoints, and leaves frozen layouts and root windows untouched. This must run on the target binding **after** its new old-coordinate step has been appended. No suffix binding receives p.
- `RenderPlan::bound_at` anchors the latest retained phase on the current output grid; its raw domain uses current physical duration and selected audio support. It does not cap growth to the frozen owner duration. `StageAudio::read_bound` permits signed retained-root coordinates through the domain transfer. Existing `bound_hidden_negative_support_is_not_capped_to_the_old_project` already covers that capability.
- Dormant SelectedPlacement returns no bound voice until its support becomes positive, preserving the stored binding. Once activated, current selection is clipped before transfer; historical empty support is not the new audible mask. Absent `Source.audio=None` must remain absent.

The proposed target windows are correct: In contraction uses `[T+d,T+L)`; extension uses `[T,T+L)` and extrapolates before that retained entry. Out requires no target-entry step. The suffix always uses `[T+L,old_total)` before the tree changes. Do not assume it exists for a terminal edit.

## Required independent PCM witnesses

Use literal coordinates and the decoded source plus reconstruction kernel as the oracle. Do not derive expected phase, selected filter support or output counts from the edited RenderPlan/binding resolver.

1. **Expose the prefix, not just rebase it.** At 30000/1001 fps and 48 kHz, `B(f)=round_even(8008*f/5)`: B(1)=1602, B(2)=3203, B(4)=6406, B(5)=8008, B(7)=11211. For an In extension of one frame at T=1, the old body moves to frame 2: its first output sample moves by **1601**, not round(1601.6)=1602. If its old Original phase is q, the new first sample extrapolates to q−1601 and the body join returns to q. For three frames at T=1, subtract B(4)−B(1)=4804. Choose measured interior context so prefix and body contain nonzero decoded PCM. Check physical prefixes 1 and 3, two chunkings, and an explicit wrong-phase negative control.
2. **Prior symbolic resume and multiple chronological steps.** Reuse `source_origin/gain/slip.rs::slip_keeps_symbolic_resume_and_chronological_reanchors_on_the_same_sample_clock`. Its independent owner entry is `64+1602+1601+1601=4868`; independent offset 7 gives Original phase 4861. Collapsing steps produces an incorrect extra sample. With the existing allocation `[2,6)` at T=4, extending In by −3 moves the retained body to frame 7 and needs physical prefix 1. The body entry must remain 4861; its extrapolated first phase is 4861−(11211−6406)=56, subject to the independently computed final support/envelope. Assert the old frozen layouts remain byte/struct equal and the inverse restores exact document and PCM.
3. **Dormant-to-audible In extension.** Put measured audio before the old W, not after it, so widening In actually activates it. Test unbound, already captured and resumed/reanchored cases. Before activation use a provider that counts or rejects all reads and assert digital zero. Afterward compute `(W' ∩ Ha) − offset` and the ceil source-sample endpoints separately; subtract a nonzero/fractional-frame offset once. Include absent-audio control. The existing Slip dormant fixture has audio after W and is not an In-extension fixture without changing that geometry.
4. **Suffix and count rounding.** Include nested ordinary siblings, compact Repeat plays plus a room-tone gap, and a nonunity Preserve suffix using `composite_insert/delete_range.rs::retained` and its cold/reverse-chunk pattern. Assert each new owner entry reproduces the old entry phase. Full block equality is appropriate only for the common allocated count: a shifted interval can gain or lose a terminal sample because B(end)−B(start) changes by one. Compute both absolute endpoints; do not require unchanged per-beat counts.
5. **44.1 kHz reconstruction and edge policy.** Reuse `composite_insert.rs::Provider`/`expected`: source coordinates advance by 147/160 per mix sample. The existing fixture's `100..20100` partial span, Blank video and independent audio are not Trim-admissible; construct a full measured-span linked fixture with a matching synthetic video clock for PCM-only evidence. Keep expected selected filter endpoints independent. Separately compare raw and edge-faded PCM for W-preserving Partition crops versus changed-W endpoints, Source Hard versus Automatic, and root sounds transformed once.

Keep provider reads at most 256 samples in these fixtures. The 48 kHz source-origin fixture contains 8197 measured samples; positive-prefix tests must remain inside that range or explicitly expect missing-input silence. No content-based alignment or widened timing tolerance.

## Scope of this conclusion

Static compatibility review only. Source-origin and Slip tests establish retained phase, offset and current-support behavior individually; they do not establish the new combined Trim command, audible In extension, root-sound transformation or atomic durability. Those remain the required execution witnesses above. No production evaluator change is justified by this review alone.
