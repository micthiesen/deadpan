# This-play fixed-Retime duration: qualification notes

The focused regression now exercises one explicit Play of a three-play Repeat, with one unclocked fixed Edit Retime directly below it. The selected Hold changes duration while the fixed Retime output and total project duration remain fixed. The fixtures cover fresh isolation and an already-owned branch, unit-rate and affine mappings in the CLI export path, plus unbound and prebound audio paths.

The checks provide useful evidence at distinct layers: core planning and inverse behavior; native service admission, visible continuation, receipt identity, undo/redo, and persistence; store macro/compound atomicity and media-preparation scope; processor-input PCM against a separate literal resampling reference; and real CLI exports with complete emitted-file picture/audio verification. Audio assertions check the selected processor instance, PointCeil entry at sample 6407, unchanged PCM outside the selected Play, and preservation of the later Repeat play and exterior owner. This verifies the sampled timing and PCM behavior in these fixtures; it is not a listening or general acoustic qualification.

The scope remains narrow. These tests do not close the complete This-play duration requirement across arbitrary nesting, multiple scoped ancestors, gap overrides, other Retime clocks or mappings, other beat combinations, all codecs, or the full editor workflow. They establish behavior for the explicit Repeat plus fixed unclocked Edit Retime shape described above, with the tested Hold and source/padding arrangements. The native UI replay uses the same bounded structural shape. No claim of full requirement closure should be made from this suite alone.
